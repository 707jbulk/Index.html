- **Massive Built-in Audience:** 25+ million active buyers

- **Global Reach:** Supports 200+ countries
- **Marketing Tools:** Built-in affiliate system, discount codes
- **Easy Integration:** Embed checkout anywhere

#### **Optimization Strategies:**
```
✅ Use high-converting product titles with power words
✅ Create compelling product descriptions (300-500 words)
✅ Upload eye-catching preview images and videos
✅ Set competitive pricing ($17-$97 sweet spot)
✅ Enable "Pay What You Want" for higher conversions
✅ Use customer ratings to build social proof
✅ Create product bundles for higher average order value
```

#### **Revenue Potential:**
- **Beginner:** $500-$2,000/month

- **Advanced:** $10,000-$50,000+/month

## **💰 PRICING STRATEGIES THAT CONVERT**

#### **Charm Pricing (ends in 7, 9):**
- $17, $27, $37, $47, $97

#### **Tiered Pricing Strategy:**
```
Basic: $47 (Core product)
Premium: $97 (Core + bonuses, 2x price)

```

#### **Bundle Pricing:**
